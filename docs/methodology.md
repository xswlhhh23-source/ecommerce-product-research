# Methodology: Product Research and Unit Economics

## Purpose
The tool structures early-stage product research. It compares assumptions consistently; it does not validate market demand or replace supplier and customer research.

## Unit economics
Platform fee = selling price × platform fee rate.

Contribution profit per unit = selling price − unit cost − shipping cost − platform fee.

Contribution margin = contribution profit ÷ selling price × 100.

Unit ROI = contribution profit ÷ (unit cost + shipping cost) × 100.

## Opportunity score
- Contribution margin: 30% (clipped to a 0–60% range for scoring)
- Estimated monthly demand: 25% (log-transformed and percentile-ranked within the dataset)
- Lower competition: 20%
- Lower return risk: 15%
- Simpler shipping: 10%

The score is relative to the products in the current dataset. If the comparison set changes, scores may change. A single-item dataset receives 100 percentile points for the demand component; interpret with care.

## Exclusions
The MVP does not include taxes, advertising, discounting, returns, storage, labor, overhead, payment settlement timing, cash conversion cycle, or inventory financing. Add these costs before making a real decision.

## Data quality
Record a source URL, capture date, currency, geography, and confidence level in a production workflow. The bundled sample is synthetic and is not market research.
